How the Model Works

Momentum Factors: measure whether a stock has recently moved upward or downward.
Volatility Factor: captures how unstable the stock has been over the last 20 trading days.
Mean-Reversion Factor: compares price against its moving average to detect overextension.
Volume Factor: detects unusual trading activity relative to recent volume.
Prediction Target: the model predicts whether each stock will outperform SPY over the next 5 trading days.
